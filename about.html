About Us

Backyard Builds is a small, family-run project born from a legacy of hands-on craftsmanship and community pride. We’re not a corporation—we’re a father-and-son (and grandpa!) team doing what we love.

Our Story

With over 30 years in construction, our dad/grandfather has spent a lifetime building homes and fixing things the right way. These days, he takes old step vans and transforms them into fully-equipped, all-purpose food trucks—ready to serve, work, and thrive.

  • Each truck is restored with attention to detail
  • Brand-new commercial kitchen installations
  • Dual split A/C units for Florida heat
  • New Predator generator installed
  • LED spotlights on all corners for night operation
